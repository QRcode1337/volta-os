Format: same as the Notion page "Scripts & Beat Maps — Batch 1 (S01–S20)" (read-only input): six beats, words ÷ 2.7 = seconds, ending variants A–D, original plus alternate hook. Word counts and beat timings below are computed, not estimated.

Sources: every script is built on one or two real documents in `/Volumes/Voltamachina/voltamachine_v3/voltamachine_v3/04-UAP/Showmeufos/` (the showmeufos.com releases, May–Oct 2026). Each one was checked against its vault note, not just the release synthesis. The vault rule holds throughout: **REAL DOCUMENT ≠ TRUE CLAIM**. Every caveat beat says what the file can't prove.

No overlap with Batch 1. S23 and S30 share the AAWSAP program with S07, and S21 sits near S19 (Tic Tac), so space those apart in the schedule.
